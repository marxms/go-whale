# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Acknowledging mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting comments, publishing private
information without permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be
considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code. They
will explain a moderation decision when it is safe to do so, and they will respect
the privacy of the reporter.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space
where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue with sensitive details for a conduct report. Use
GitHub's private reporting on this repository (**Security → Report a
vulnerability**) and mark the report as a conduct report; it will be handled
separately from a security disclosure. If the report involves the only maintainer,
say so explicitly so the escalation is visible. If the private form is unavailable,
open a minimal issue asking for a private contact and do not include the details.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction** — a private or public warning that explains the impact and the
   expected behaviour.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour and a period
   of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from interaction with the project.
